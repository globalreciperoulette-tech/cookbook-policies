1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Global Recipe Roulette (the “App”) handles information when you download, access, or use the App.

For purposes of this Policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the developer or operator of Global Recipe Roulette. “You” refers to any person who uses the App.

The App may provide international recipe discovery, random recipe selection, cooking instructions, user-created recipes, ratings, shopping lists, photographs, achievements, Culinary Passport features, advertising, and third-party integrations.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Policy. Where applicable law requires consent to a particular type of data processing, this acknowledgment does not replace the separate consent that may be required.

The information handled depends on the features you use, your device, your settings, and the third-party services integrated into the version of the App you use.

A. Information you enter or create

The App may process information you choose to enter, including:

  • Recipes, ingredients, cooking instructions, and recipe notes.
  • Recipe ratings, favorites, and preferences.
  • Shopping lists and cooking-related information.
  • Photographs you select or add.
  • Culinary Passport progress, stamps, achievements, and similar game-related information.
  • App settings and advertising privacy preferences.

Some information may be stored on your device. If a feature transfers information to another service, that transfer is subject to the feature's operation and the relevant disclosures.

Do not enter sensitive personal information that is not necessary for using the App.

B. Information processed by advertising services

The App may use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to display advertisements and manage applicable consent choices.

Depending on the SDK, configuration, device, region, and consent settings, Google or its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • IP address.

  • App interactions and advertising interactions.
  • Advertising delivery, measurement, and performance data.

The App's advertising services may process information automatically when the relevant SDKs operate. The developer does not necessarily receive or control all information processed directly by these third parties.

Google's disclosures regarding its Mobile Ads SDK are available at Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
